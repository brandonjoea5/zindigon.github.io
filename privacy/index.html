Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

First draft for review, not final legal copy. This page covers the whole zindigon.com site (games, DCUO tools, Zindigon League, and Bloodline Zero) in plain language. Please read it over and edit anything that isn't accurate before treating it as the live policy, and have a lawyer review it if you plan to rely on it.

This policy explains what happens to your information across zindigon.com and its games, tools, and stories. Individual products may link to this page or keep their own product-specific policy (for example ToonData's own privacy page); where they differ, the product-specific page governs that product.

The short version

Most of Zindigon's tools (ToonData, Builds, Zindigon League's lookup features) don't require an account and don't store personal data beyond standard hosting logs. Some features do require an account, sign-in, or payment: Zindigon League's Plus and Premier subscriptions, and Bloodline Zero's paid episodes and My Library. Those use Supabase for accounts and Stripe for payment processing, described below.

Accounts and sign-in

Where an account is offered (Zindigon League, Bloodline Zero, and any other product that shows a Sign In option), account data (email address and a hashed password) is handled by Supabase, our authentication and database provider. We don't see or store your password ourselves. A signed-in session is shared across Zindigon products on this domain so you don't have to sign in separately for each one.

Payments

Paid features, such as Zindigon League Plus/Premier subscriptions and individual Bloodline Zero episodes, are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered directly into Stripe's own checkout and are never sent to or stored on Zindigon's servers. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and which product it was for, not your card number.

Game data and leaderboards

Games with cloud saves or leaderboards (for example Elemental Rift) store gameplay progress and leaderboard scores through Supabase, tied to an account or an anonymous device identifier depending on the game. This data is limited to what's needed to save your progress and show scores; it isn't sold or shared with advertisers.

AI features

Some tools include an AI assistant (for example Zindigon League's match insights chat). Messages you send to an AI feature are sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate a response and are not used to identify you personally beyond what's needed to answer your question in that session.

Analytics

Zindigon uses Google Analytics on parts of the site to understand traffic, such as which pages are visited and roughly how many people play each game. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technology. It does not have access to your account email, password, or payment details.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. Both may automatically collect standard technical information common to any web server, such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps, as part of operating their infrastructure. See GitHub's and Cloudflare's own privacy statements for details on how they handle this data.

Advertising

Some pages may show ads served through an ad network. If and where ads are shown, the ad provider may use cookies or similar technology to personalize ads and measure performance. This section will be updated to name the specific provider and any consent controls required for your location before ads are enabled on a given page.

Data retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Game saves and leaderboard entries are retained for as long as the game is live. Payment records are retained by Stripe according to Stripe's own retention rules and applicable financial recordkeeping requirements.

Children's privacy

Zindigon's games and tools are not directed at children and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age required by their local law to consent to data collection. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, contact us and we'll address it.

Your options

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you (account details, game saves, purchase history) by contacting us below. We'll respond and confirm once it's done.

Future changes

As Zindigon adds features, this policy will be updated to accurately describe what's collected, why, and how long it's kept, before those features go live. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to contact@zindigon.com.
