Quick rules reference
  • 5 Mana every turn (no ramp). 5-card starting hand, draw 1/turn.
  • 3 Mana → draw a card, any number of times per turn.
  • 5 stacks per side, 3 cards max per stack.
  • Only the top card of a stack is exposed: it can Basic Attack once/turn and can be targeted.
  • Buried cards are protected but (in this vanilla-only prototype) have no Active ability to use yet.
  • New cards can't attack the turn they're played (no retaliation on Basic Attacks).
  • Dig lets a played card slot in beneath the current top card instead of on top.
  • Reduce the enemy Player to 0 Health to win.
