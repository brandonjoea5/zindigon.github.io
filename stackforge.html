Quick rules reference
  • 5 Mana every turn (no ramp). 5-card starting hand, draw 1/turn. 3 Mana → draw a card, any number of times per turn.
  • 5 stacks per side, 3 cards max per stack. Only the Top card is Exposed (can Basic Attack, can be targeted). Buried cards are protected and can use an Active ability instead.
  • New cards, new Evolutions, and Revived cards can't act the turn they enter play. Basic Attacks cause no retaliation. Card Health damage is permanent.
  • Only cards with the Dig keyword (Goblin King, Grylyn) can be placed beneath another card — everything else always goes on top, burying whatever was there.
  • Evolution Pool: lives outside the normal deck. 3 living Goblins on your board auto-consumes them and pulls Goblin King from the pool.
  • Shield absorbs damage before Health. DoT ticks at the end of the caster's own turns. Weakened adds +1 per damage instance that reaches Health.
  • Revive returns a card to the top of the stack it died in (unless a card says otherwise) and re-triggers its On Reveal. A full stack blocks that specific Revive — no searching for another spot.
  • Gain Health raises current AND max Health permanently. Restore only heals up to the existing max.
  • Bucket +N grants +N Mana on the very next turn only. Cooldowns block re-use for the stated number of the controller's own turns.
  • A 0-Health card's On Reveal still resolves in full before it dies (e.g. Sacrifice).
  • Reduce the enemy Player to 0 Health to win.
