Elemental Rift — Free Browser RPG

Master the Elements. Defeat Waves of Enemies. Challenge the World Boss.

Elemental Rift is a free online browser RPG developed by Zindigon Enterprises. No download required — play instantly in any web browser on desktop or mobile.

Choose Your Class

Pick from three classes: Mage, Warrior, or Rogue. Each class has unique abilities, playstyles, and imbue combinations.

  • Mage — Spell caster with Spell Vamp. Choose Fire, Water, Grass, Rock, or Wind elemental imbue.
  • Warrior — Sword and Shield with Life Steal. Choose Poison, Electric, Ice, Rock, or Wind imbue.
  • Rogue — Dual daggers or bow with Life Steal. Choose Poison, Electric, Ice, Rock, or Wind imbue.

Elemental Abilities

Master eight elemental types: Fire (burn damage over time), Water (slows and weakens enemies), Grass (heals on hit), Poison (stacking DOT), Electric (AOE chain damage), Ice (freeze and slow), Rock (stuns and defense shredding), and Wind (attack speed and evasion).

Game Features

  • Wave survival combat against increasingly powerful enemies
  • Gear system with Basic, Great, Exquisite, Ancient, and Cosmic tiers
  • Skill tree with passive upgrades across Offense, Defense, Effects, and Utility
  • Trinity Force cosmic progression system with persistent unlocks
  • Global leaderboard — compete for the highest World Boss damage
  • Multiple difficulty modes: Easy, Medium, Hard, and Extreme
  • Free to play — sign in to save progress, or play as a guest
